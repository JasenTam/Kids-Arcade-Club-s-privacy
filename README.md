# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

## Kids Arcade Club

Kids Arcade Club is a children's puzzle game app. We respect user privacy and keep the app simple and safe for families.

## Personal Information

Kids Arcade Club does not ask for, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require account registration and does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or other personal data.

## Local Game Data

The app may store game progress locally on the device, such as:

- Recently played games
- Favorite games
- Play counts
- Win counts
- Best scores
- Fastest times
- Fewest moves
- Haptics preference
- Sound effects preference

This information is stored only on the user's device and is not sent to us or to any third party.

## Analytics

Kids Arcade Club uses Firebase Analytics to understand general app and gameplay events, such as:

- App launches
- Game opens
- Round completion and success events
- Favorite changes
- Best score and fastest time updates
- Monster Cannon gameplay actions

These analytics events do not include names, contact details, precise location, photos, contacts, or account identifiers created by this app.

## Children’s Privacy

Kids Arcade Club is designed for children, but it does not collect personal information from children.

## Third-Party Services

Kids Arcade Club uses Firebase Analytics for basic app usage analytics. The app does not use advertising SDKs, social login services, or in-app behavioral advertising.

## Internet Access

Kids Arcade Club does not require internet access for gameplay.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this file with an updated date.
